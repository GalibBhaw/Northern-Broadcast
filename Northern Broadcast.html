About Northern Broadcast

Northern Broadcast is an event management and concert organizing agency based in Naogaon, Bangladesh.

We plan, coordinate and execute concerts, farewell festivals, cultural programs, college and university events, private events and special occasions.

We work with artists, bands, venues, production teams, sponsors, volunteers and event partners to bring ideas from concept to execution.
